Progression is competency-based, not dependent only on course completion or years of experience. Level 5 experience expectations are indicative; demonstrated capability and project evidence determine progression.

3.1 Three learning and practice buckets

Bucket A — Discover & Analyse (Student to FDI)

  • Business fundamentals and value chains
  • Domain awareness and business functions
  • Stakeholder engagement and process observation
  • Process mapping, evidence collection and validation
  • Problem identification, prioritisation and documentation

Outcome: ability to conduct supervised business discovery and document operational realities.

Bucket B — Engineer & Deploy (FDI / FDTE to FDE)

  • Requirements engineering and data discovery
  • APIs, systems integration and technical architecture
  • AI, LLMs, RAG and Agentic AI
  • Application and software engineering
  • Testing, deployment, reliability and production operations

Outcome: ability to engineer, integrate, test and deploy solutions for defined business problems.

Bucket C — Architect & Transform (FDE to Solution Consultant)

  • Complex business transformation and process architecture
  • Business case and economic modelling
  • Solution alternatives and technology trade-offs
  • Multi-stakeholder alignment and change management
  • Enterprise deployment governance and value realisation

Outcome: ability to architect and lead complex business–technology transformation initiatives.

3.3 Curriculum and learning philosophy

The curriculum balances two dimensions: 50% Operational Excellence (technology, data, engineering, architecture, integration, testing and deployment) and 50% Business Alignment & Change Excellence (discovery, process understanding, stakeholder alignment, operating-model change, adoption and value realisation). These are curriculum design proportions, not a prescribed division of project effort.

Initial learning design hypothesis: 20–30% targeted learning and coaching, and 70–80% practice, simulation and assessed project work. Validate this through pilot cohorts.

The GCODE Deployment Practice Lab will provide three environments: (1) 360-degree Discovery Simulator; (2) Engineering & Deployment Lab; and (3) Enterprise Transformation War Room.
