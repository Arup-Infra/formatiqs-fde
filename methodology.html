Integrated FDE Delivery Methodology

FormatiQs will differentiate itself through a business-first, engineering-led delivery methodology.

Stage 1 — Touch-and-Go Discovery

Understand the business pulse through stakeholder conversations, ground-level observation and high-level value-chain discovery.

Stage 2 — Value-Chain Affinitisation

Identify interconnected processes, dependencies, hand-offs, stakeholders and business outcomes.

Stage 3 — Process Rationalisation

Evaluate process elements through four decisions: Remove, Replace, Repair or Retain.

Stage 4 — Detailed Process Architecture

Develop As-Is and To-Be process architecture, maps, requirements, controls, dependencies and operating-model considerations.

Stage 5 — Technical & Data Architecture

Understand data inputs and outputs, systems, APIs, interfaces, technical constraints, security and infrastructure requirements.

Stage 6 — Integrated Solution Architecture & Wireframing

Combine process and technical architecture to determine holistic versus plug-in solutions, build versus buy, in-house versus external sourcing, and AI, automation or conventional software options.

Stage 7 — Business Case & Development Feasibility

Compare current and future costs, benefits, investment, risks, feasibility and expected returns.

Stage 8 — Build, Deploy, Adopt & Realise Value

Engineer, integrate, test, deploy, enable business adoption and measure outcomes.

8.1 Evidence-led discovery

FormatiQs will distinguish observed facts, stakeholder-reported information, validated data and unvalidated assumptions. Where business access is limited, discovery gaps must be documented and deeper access sought through the appropriate client sponsor. Technology decisions should be based on business evidence rather than assumptions.

Quality, Governance & Performance Framework

14.1 Talent performance

  • Assessment completion and competency achievement
  • Practical project scores
  • Deployment readiness
  • Client feedback
  • Progression between FDE levels

14.2 Engineering performance

  • Solution quality and integration success
  • Defect and rework rates
  • Testing coverage
  • Security and reliability
  • Deployment success and documentation quality

14.3 Business outcome performance

  • Process cycle-time and productivity improvement
  • Cost, error and rework reduction
  • User adoption
  • AI utilisation and solution effectiveness
  • Business value realised

14.4 Commercial performance

  • Qualified leads and paid discovery engagements
  • Academy enrolments and assessment-to-deployment conversion
  • Talent deployment utilisation
  • Project gross margin and recurring revenue
  • Client retention and expansion

Outcome metrics must have agreed baselines, measurement methods and clearly defined client responsibilities.

14.5 Governance structure

  • Strategic Steering Committee
  • FDE Factory Head
  • Academy & Assessment Lead
  • Talent Sourcing Lead
  • Solution Architecture Lead
  • Engineering & Delivery Lead
  • Quality & Governance Lead
  • Client Engagement / Account Lead

Initial operations can combine multiple responsibilities within a lean team.
