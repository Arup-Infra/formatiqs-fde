GCODE connects and orchestrates. FormatiQs engineers and delivers. Partners contribute specialist technology, domain knowledge, training, infrastructure and delivery capacity.

Commercial arrangements, lead ownership, IP, delivery responsibility and revenue sharing must be documented in partner-specific agreements.

Partner Ecosystem Strategy

Technology partners: AI platforms, cloud providers, enterprise software and development tools.

Process architecture partners: BPM, workflow modelling and process transformation.

Core engineering partners: AI, full-stack, application and software engineering.

Data and integration partners: data engineering, cloud and enterprise integration.

Domain partners: BFSI, manufacturing, healthcare, education, retail and other specialists.

People and change partners: change management, organisational transformation and adoption.

Academic partners: universities, colleges and technical institutions.

Training and assessment partners: learning content, certification and competency evaluation.

Simulation partners: industry scenarios, practical exercises and deployment simulations.

Global deployment partners: international market access, local delivery and client relationship support.

Partner evaluation should cover technical capability, domain expertise, delivery track record, quality, commercial viability, IP and confidentiality commitments, security and compliance readiness, and scalability. Where FormatiQs contracts as prime provider, it remains the accountable integrator.
